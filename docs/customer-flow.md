1. Customer approaches pool table with smart device
2. Customer selects number of games on device keypad/display
3. Device shows amount to pay (e.g., 500 RWF per game)
4. Customer makes mobile money payment (MoMo/Airtel)
5. Device validates payment and adds games to customer's account
6. Customer presses "Play" button
7. Device unlocks ball compartment via motorized lock
8. Customer takes balls and plays pool
9. Device tracks remaining games
10. When games complete, device locks compartment
